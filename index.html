Kiwi horizions X Save the kiwi

Our mission is to give kiwi a future — and you can be part of it. By donating, you help fund predator control, breeding programs, safe habitats, and rescue efforts that directly protect these vulnerable birds. Every contribution brings us one step closer to a world where kiwi no longer face extinction. Every week, around 20 kiwi birds are killed, leaving only 68,000 left in declining numbers. But there is hope. Thanks teh dedication and conservation efforts from the support of the people who care. 454 kiwi eggs have been hatched, and 333 kiwi birds have been recollected to their safe, forever homes where they can thrive.
